# About & Editorial Policy

## Who we are

Fishing Gear Reviews is an independent buying-guide site for US anglers. We compare real rods, reels, electronics and tackle so you can buy with confidence — focused on what performs on the water, not what reads well in a spec sheet.

## How we write

Our guides are built from a combination of:/n- Manufacturer specifications and published product documentation
- Aggregated owner feedback and long-term user reports
- Published independent testing standards for the category

Where a spec or price is uncertain, we mark it as needing verification rather than guessing. We do not fabricate test results, awards, ratings, or quotes. Prices shown are typical street ranges and fluctuate with promotions. Guides are updated as products change.

## Affiliate disclosure

Fishing Gear Reviews participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and fishing-specific affiliate programs (retailers and manufacturers). We earn commissions from qualifying purchases made through links on this site. This never affects our recommendations: verdicts are chosen on merit, and a higher commission rate does not buy a better ranking in our comparisons.

## Corrections

Spotted an error? We fix it. Corrections and updates are reflected directly in the articles.
